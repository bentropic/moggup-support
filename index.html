Frequently asked questions about MoggUp.

How do I play MoggUp?

Swipe on the 4 × 4 board. Two matching tiles merge into the next evolution. A move that changes nothing does not spawn a tile. You can keep playing beyond 2048.

Where is my progress saved?

Your game, coins, gems, unlocked sets and settings are saved on this device. Cross-device cloud sync is not currently included. Do not delete the app as your first troubleshooting step: deleting it may remove local data.

How do I change the pictures or sounds?

Open the set picker in the main menu to choose an unlocked picture set. More sets are available in the shop. Fruit and numbers are the free standard sets. You can choose sounds independently from pictures.

A purchase or Premium is missing. What should I do?

Check your internet connection and make sure you are using the Apple Account that made the purchase. For Premium, open the Premium screen and select “Restore purchases”. You can also restart the app. If purchased gems are missing, contact support before purchasing again. Restoring Premium does not restore spent gems or local game progress.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → MoggUp. You can manage or cancel your subscription there, or use “Manage subscription” on the Premium screen. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles refund requests for App Store purchases. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and follow the available steps. Apple determines refund eligibility.

Why can’t I hear any sounds?

Enable “Merge sounds” in MoggUp settings. Check media volume, silent mode and connected headphones. A merge sound plays when two matching tiles actually combine.

My video export is not working.

Keep MoggUp open while rendering and check available storage. To save a video to Photos, allow adding photos in iOS Settings. Try exporting again and note any error message if the problem continues. Sharing to TikTok is a separate step after exporting.

What should I do if the app crashes?

Reopen MoggUp and check for an app update. Note the action that caused the app to close and contact support. Avoid deleting the app first so that your local data is preserved.
