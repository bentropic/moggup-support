Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data the MoggUp iPhone app processes. You do not need an account to play.

Who is responsible

Stored on your device

Your game, best score, coins, gems, unlocked sets and settings stay on your iPhone. A counter of your free video exports is kept in the iOS Keychain so that it survives a reinstall. We have no server for this data and cannot see it (except for inviting friends, see below).

Videos

Highlight videos are rendered on your iPhone. They leave the device only if you share them yourself, for example to TikTok. The service you choose is then responsible for them.

Inviting friends

Only when you open “Invite friends” does MoggUp create an invite account on our invite server (Cloudflare, Inc., USA): a random player ID, a secret key (stored only as a hash) and your invite code. When you redeem a code, we store who invited whom and how many coins you have earned by playing since then, so we can pay the bonus. To prevent abuse we use Apple’s DeviceCheck to ask whether a code was already redeemed on your iPhone and to mark it. Apple keeps two bits per device for this, which say nothing about you; we do not store the device token itself. We do not collect your name, email or contacts. We delete your invite account on request; just email us your code.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, operated by Google Ireland Limited. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first using Google's consent form. iOS separately asks whether apps may track you. If you decline tracking, the advertising identifier is not shared and the game stays fully playable.

According to Google's own declaration, the SDK can process: the device advertising identifier (for tracking, only with your permission), coarse location, ad data, product interaction, performance data, crash data and other diagnostics. The purposes are advertising and analytics. You can change your consent in Settings → Privacy choices in the app, and tracking in the iOS Settings app. While Premium is active, the ad SDK is not started when the app launches. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

Usage and ad measurement (Google Analytics for Firebase)

From version 1.0.5, Google Analytics for Firebase replaces ByteBrew. Collection is off until you explicitly allow “Usage and ad measurement”. With consent, Google processes app-instance identifiers, app starts and sessions, device/app information, approximate location derived from connection data, and purchase events to help us understand usage and measure our Google Ads campaigns. Google Ireland Limited provides this service; processing may also take place in the USA. Advertising identifiers and advertising-data consent are enabled only with your additional iOS tracking permission. This measurement choice does not enable personalized advertising. It applies independently of Premium. You can withdraw it in Settings → Usage and ad measurement; collection stops and the local Analytics identifier is reset. Previously transmitted data is not automatically erased by this switch. See Google’s privacy policy and Firebase privacy information.

Earlier app versions: ByteBrew

We also promote MoggUp with ads inside other games (Unity Ads). To see which ad led to an install, we use the SDK from ByteBrew (USA). It only starts once you have agreed to Google's consent form and allowed tracking in iOS; otherwise MoggUp sends nothing to ByteBrew. It processes the device's advertising identifier, a user ID assigned by ByteBrew, device model, mobile carrier, app version and session data. ByteBrew matches the install with Unity Ads and only tells Unity that it came from our ad. You can withdraw tracking in the iOS settings; collection stops at the next app launch. See ByteBrew's privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions and gem packs are handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details. See Apple's privacy policy.

Support

If you email us, we use your address and message only to answer you and delete them once they are no longer needed. Email is provided by Google (Gmail). This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which processes technical access data including your IP address.

Legal basis

Analytics, ads and tracking rely on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), purchases and inviting friends on the contract (Art. 6(1)(b)), and support and security on our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, restriction and portability, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. You may also complain to a data protection authority. Deleting the app removes the local game data; the Keychain counter contains only a month and a number.

Children

MoggUp is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We update this policy when the app changes. The date below shows the current version.
