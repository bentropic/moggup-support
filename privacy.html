Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data the MoggUp iPhone app processes. You do not need an account to play.

Who is responsible

Berkan Beser, c/o POSTFLEX PFX-221-511, Emsdettener Straße 10, 48268 Greven, Germany, besertrade@gmail.com

Stored on your device

Your game, best score, coins, gems, unlocked sets and settings stay on your iPhone. A counter of your free video exports is kept in the iOS Keychain so that it survives a reinstall. We have no server for this data and cannot see it.

Videos

Highlight videos are rendered on your iPhone. They leave the device only if you share them yourself, for example to TikTok. The service you choose is then responsible for them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, operated by Google Ireland Limited. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first using Google's consent form. iOS separately asks whether apps may track you. If you decline tracking, the advertising identifier is not shared and the game stays fully playable.

According to Google's own declaration, the SDK can process: the device advertising identifier (for tracking, only with your permission), coarse location, ad data, product interaction, performance data, crash data and other diagnostics. The purposes are advertising and analytics. You can change your consent in Settings → Privacy choices in the app, and tracking in the iOS Settings app. While Premium is active, the ad SDK is not started when the app launches. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

Purchases

Subscriptions and gem packs are handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details. See Apple's privacy policy.

Support

If you email us, we use your address and message only to answer you and delete them once they are no longer needed. Email is provided by Google (Gmail). This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which processes technical access data including your IP address.

Legal basis

Ads and tracking rely on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), purchases on the contract (Art. 6(1)(b)), and support and security on our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, restriction and portability, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. You may also complain to a data protection authority. Deleting the app removes the local game data; the Keychain counter contains only a month and a number.

Children

MoggUp is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We update this policy when the app changes. The date below shows the current version.
